# ENGPHYS 4Z04 – Semiconductor Manufacturing Technology

## Course Description

An advanced course covering the theory and practice of semiconductor microfabrication and nanofabrication technologies used in modern semiconductor manufacturing.

Students study the complete semiconductor manufacturing process, from wafer production through device fabrication and assembly. The course combines classroom instruction, computer-based process simulation, and cleanroom laboratory experience. Students also use industry-standard semiconductor process simulation tools to model fabrication processes and analyze device performance. 【1-01a8d5】

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

- Explain the major processes used in semiconductor manufacturing.
- Analyze fabrication workflows for microelectronic and nanoelectronic devices.
- Apply mathematical and physical models to semiconductor fabrication processes.
- Use process simulation software to model semiconductor manufacturing.
- Design fabrication process flows for semiconductor devices.
- Evaluate fabrication constraints, assumptions, and tradeoffs.
- Analyze device performance resulting from fabrication decisions.
- Communicate technical concepts through written reports and oral presentations.
- Work effectively in laboratory and project teams. 【1-01a8d5】

## Laboratory Component

The laboratory component focuses on solar-cell microfabrication within a semiconductor cleanroom environment.

Students gain experience with:

- Cleanroom operation and safety
- Semiconductor process workflows
- Microfabrication techniques
- Group-based laboratory projects
- Experimental process characterization

## Computational Component

Students use industry-standard semiconductor process simulation software to:

- Simulate semiconductor fabrication processes
- Model photovoltaic device structures
- Develop virtual process flows
- Analyze device characteristics
- Evaluate fabrication-performance relationships

## Active Learning Elements

### Team-Based Oral Presentation

Student teams investigate the application of semiconductor devices currently used in industry or under active research.

Topics connect device functionality with the manufacturing techniques studied throughout the course.

### Project-Based Simulation

Students design and simulate a PERL solar cell using professional semiconductor simulation software and prepare a technical report analyzing the resulting device performance.

### Laboratory Project

Students fabricate and analyze semiconductor structures in the cleanroom environment and complete an individual technical report.
