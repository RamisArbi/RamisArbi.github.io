# ENGPHYS 4UM2 – Modern and Applied Physics Laboratory: Nano- and Micro-Devices (Solar)

## Course Description

An advanced laboratory course focused on semiconductor device fabrication through the design, fabrication, testing, and characterization of photovoltaic solar cells.

Students gain practical experience with fabrication techniques used in modern microelectronics and renewable energy technologies while learning how device design influences performance.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

- Explain the operating principles of silicon solar cells.
- Understand the major fabrication steps used in semiconductor manufacturing.
- Simulate solar-cell device behavior.
- Fabricate functioning photovoltaic devices.
- Characterize and interpret experimental device performance.
- Communicate technical results through formal laboratory reports.

## Laboratory Topics

### Fabrication

- Introduction to semiconductor processing
- Photolithography
- Surface texturing
- Dopant diffusion and doping
- Anti-reflection coatings
- Back-side metallization
- Front-side metallization
- Device testing and characterization

### Simulation

- Device structure definition
- Meshing strategies
- Electrical contacts
- Device parameter analysis
- Performance simulation

## Course Structure
