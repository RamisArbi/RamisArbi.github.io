# ENGPHYS 3ES3 – Introduction to Energy Systems

## Course Description

A survey course on energy systems with emphasis on the analytical tools needed to evaluate energy technologies in terms of performance, resource requirements, environmental sustainability, economic factors, and life-cycle impacts.

The course examines the scientific, technological, societal, and environmental dimensions of energy production, conversion, storage, transportation, and consumption. Particular emphasis is placed on sustainability, climate change, and emerging energy technologies.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course, students are expected to:

- Analyze technological and human factors that influence energy systems.
- Evaluate carbon footprints and sustainability metrics of energy technologies.
- Assess environmental, societal, and economic tradeoffs associated with energy systems.
- Independently research and synthesize information related to emerging energy technologies.

## Major Topics

- Energy fundamentals
- Energy and exergy
- Centralized vs. decentralized energy systems
- Energy storage technologies
- Human factors in energy systems
- Solar energy
- Wind energy
- Hydroelectric energy
- Nuclear energy
- Combustion-based energy systems
- Alternative energy technologies
- Socio-economic and environmental impacts
- Global energy disparities
- Sustainability and climate change

## Course Features

- Student-led energy technology presentations
- Project idea development and pitching
- Analysis of sustainability tradeoffs
- Evaluation of global energy challenges
- Integration of technical and societal perspectives

## Accreditation Outcomes

The course contributes to development of student abilities related to:

- Engineering and society
- Sustainability and environmental stewardship
- Societal impact assessment
- Long-term engineering decision making
