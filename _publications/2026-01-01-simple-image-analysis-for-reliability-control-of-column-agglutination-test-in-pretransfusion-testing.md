Objectives: A novel meniscus-based analysis provides a rapid method to identify analytical errors related to unrecognised pipetting errors. Column agglutination technique-based indirect antiglobulin testing depends on correct separation of liquids by an air gap; pipetting errors can compromise this separation and lead to false-negative or weak results. The study used analyser images from 132 simulated crossmatch tests and found high discriminatory performance (AUC 0.935). Further validation across platforms and laboratories is needed.
