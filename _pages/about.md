I am an applied physicist working on thin film based sensors for ions. My experience includes self-assembly of inorganic nanoparticles, computational physics and chemistry, ceramics and a boatload of characterization methods including optical spectroscopy, interferometry, vibrational spectroscopy, force microscopy, electron microscopy and voltammetry. I have also gotten my hands dirty with automatic particle detection using computer vision programs in MATLAB and python and CAD for 3D printed parts.
