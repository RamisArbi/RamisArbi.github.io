I am an applied physicist working on thin film based sensors for ions. My experience includes self-assembly of inorganic nanoparticles, computational physics and chemistry, ceramics and a boatload of characterization methods including optical spectroscopy, interferometry, vibrational spectroscopy, force microscopy, electron microscopy and voltammetry. I have also gotten my hands dirty with automatic particle detection in optical and electron microscopy using computer vision in MATLAB and python. I have built a suite of high throughput data analysis of emission, absorbance and time-resolved spectroscopy during my postdoc at Niko Hildebrandt's lab in Python. In my free time, I enjoy tinkering with (and, if luck has me, fixing) broken electronics equipment, watches and computers.
